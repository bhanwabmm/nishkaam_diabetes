About the Clinic

Who We Are

Nishkaam Diabetes Care is a dedicated diabetes centre in Kavi Nagar, Ghaziabad, led by Dr. Prahlad Chawla. We focus on one thing and do it exceptionally well — helping people manage and reverse the impact of diabetes through personalized, evidence-based treatment.

From your very first visit, our team looks at the complete picture: your medical history, lifestyle, diet, and daily routine. We combine modern health monitoring with practical guidance and patient education so you're never managing your condition alone.

  • Treatment plans tailored to each individual, not a one-size-fits-all approach
  • Diet & lifestyle support that actually fits your life
  • Modern monitoring to track progress and adjust early
  • On-site pharmacy so you leave with everything you need
  • Ongoing education so you understand and own your health

Complete Investigations — All Under One Roof

No running around to different labs. From nerve and circulation checks to eye and foot screening, everything happens right here.

Blood Sugar

HbA1c Test

Your true 3-month average blood sugar in a single, reliable number.

Circulation

Ankle Brachial Index (ABI)

Checks blood flow to your legs to catch artery blockages (PAD) early.

Nerve Health

Vibration Perception Threshold

Measures how well your feet sense vibration — an early warning sign of nerve damage.

Nerve Health

Digital Biothesiometer

Precise, digital measurement of foot nerve sensitivity to track neuropathy over time.

Eye Screening

Fundoscopy

A close look at the retina to detect diabetic eye damage before it affects vision.

Heart Health

ECG

A quick heart check — because diabetes and heart health go hand in hand.

Foot Pressure

Podiascan

A digital scan of your foot's pressure points to spot ulcer-prone areas early.

Foot Pressure

Harris Mat / Foot Imprinter

A foot imprint that reveals uneven pressure and gait issues before they cause harm.

Foot Care

Callus & Corn Removal

Professional foot care that keeps small problems from becoming serious wounds.

Questions, Answered

A few things patients often ask before their first visit.

  • Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

    You're welcome to walk in during clinic hours, but we recommend messaging us on WhatsApp or calling ahead so we can reserve a time and keep your wait short.

  • What types of diabetes do you treat?

    We manage Type 1, Type 2, pre-diabetes, and gestational (pregnancy-related) diabetes, along with related metabolic and lifestyle concerns.

  • Should I bring anything to my first visit?

    If you have them, please bring recent blood reports, a list of current medications, and any past prescriptions. If not, don't worry — we can arrange the necessary tests here.

  • Do you provide diet and lifestyle guidance?

    Yes. Every treatment plan includes practical diet and lifestyle support designed to fit your routine, not disrupt it.

  • Is a pharmacy available at the clinic?

    Yes — we have on-site pharmacy access, so you can collect your prescribed medicines and supplies before you leave.
